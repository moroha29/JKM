## Direction contract

THESIS: JKM reads as a Singapore civic wayfinding system (HDB block directory boards, MRT/estate signage) — not another dark-mode neon "digital studio" template — because the studio's real claim is helping people find their way through a complex decision, which is literally true of all four case studies.

OWN-WORLD: Matte civic-signage enamel palette at full-page scale — forest green and navy panels, warm cream/off-white lettering plates, one reserved safety-amber for the single primary action (email CTA, active nav state) — never scattered as a decorative accent. Archivo (Black/Expanded for oversized directional headlines, Regular/Medium for body) paired with JetBrains Mono for all tabular figures, stat strips, and case-study fact numbers, rendered as odometer/digit-bank panels that click into place. A faint visible construction grid runs under signage panels (Crouwel raise), literalizing "the boring parts are the trustworthy parts."

STORY: A visitor — cold or warm — lands, reads one oversized cropped directional word as the hero's "door" (Scher raise), sees an actual directory of the four real, named builds (RexSG, Moof, mySOS, Website Manager) each as a numbered directory entry with a live link where one truthfully exists, believes the studio ships real production systems (not concepts), and finds a single amber "email us" action always one tap away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed signboard: studio wordmark as a block-directory plate top-left, "Taking on select projects" as a lit indicator, oversized cropped directional headline as the one shouted word/arrow, a compact facts strip (digit-bank style) beneath naming the four real builds by number, primary amber CTA visible without scrolling.

FORM: Assigned direction, index 5 (HDB/civic wayfinding signage) from the grounded list of 7 Singapore-vernacular candidates, raised with donations from Crouwel-grid-specimen (visible construction grid), signals-instruments-nixie-laboratory-counter (odometer-style fact panels), brand-identity-canon-scher-street-typography (one oversized cropped hero word), and kinetic-sculpture-automata-crank-paper-menagerie (tactile flip-tab nav/directory micro-interactions). Declined outright: dream-surreal-impossible-worlds-sleeping-city-fold and webgl-shader-portal (the latter is explicitly the category rut for "ambitious digital studio" sites — full-bleed shader, custom cursor, percentage loader — and was rejected on product-clarity grounds: it can't hold dense, evidence-led case-study content).
