Moof

A warm, easy-to-run website for a small matcha bar in Singapore.

Matcha & hojicha · Shenton Way

What they needed

A new café wanted a website that felt as inviting as the shop—without being stuck with a look that did not suit it.

6
different website looks tried
1
favourite chosen and launched

The key idea

Rather than guess what the owner would like, we made six different versions of the website using the café’s real menu and photos. The owner clicked through them all and picked a favourite—and that one became the real website.

Read the full story

The problem

A young brand still finding its style.

The café needed a website that felt warm and personal, while keeping the menu, photos, opening hours, and story easy to change.

What we did

Six options, one clear favourite.

Each version had its own look and feel. The one the owner chose uses bold lettering, warm photos of the counter, a drinks menu you can swipe through, and simple navigation.

The result

A fast website the owner can update.

The chosen design is now the café’s website. The team changes drinks, prices, words, and photos themselves using Website Manager—no developer needed.
